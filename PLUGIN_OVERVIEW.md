Follow and steer your BB agent threads from Slack, with your phone in mind.

## What you get

- **A live status card per thread.** It shows the agent's current step, plan and latest update while it works, and updates in place so it never buzzes your phone. In the Slack DM list, the cards double as a dashboard of everything running.
- **One notification per turn.** The agent's final reply arrives as a Slack message with the thread's name, formatted natively: headings, lists, code blocks, tables and links.
- **Questions and approvals as buttons.** Answer a question with a tap, or approve or deny a command after reading it in full.
- **Talk back from Slack.** Reply in the thread to steer the agent. Images, files and voice memos come along; voice memos are transcribed.
- **Start work from Slack.** Send the app a message to start a new BB thread.
- **Controls in BB.** Turn Slackoff on or off from the sidebar, and mirror any thread from its header.

## How it works

Slackoff connects to a Slack app you own over Socket Mode, so BB needs no public address. Tokens stay on the BB server as secret settings. Only your Slack account can control threads.
